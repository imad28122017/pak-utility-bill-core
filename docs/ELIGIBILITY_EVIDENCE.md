## Qualification paths (as published November 2026)

- Maintainer: >=500 dependent repositories OR >=100 dependent packages OR >=200,000 monthly downloads aggregated across registries.
- Active contributor: >=100 genuinely merged PRs into public repos the applicant does not own in last 12 months.
- Community builder: >=20 unique external contributors with merged PRs to one maintained repo in last 12 months.
- Core recognized-project committer or OpenSSF criticality >=0.4.
- Discretionary ecosystem impact: independent, demonstrable reliance on a maintained library.

All general requirements (GitHub 2+ years, public activity last 90 days, OSI license, age 18+, permitted residency and others) still apply; satisfying thresholds is not a guarantee.
